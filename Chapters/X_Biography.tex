Matheus Kunzler Maldaner earned his Bachelor’s degree in Data Science from the University of Florida, where he developed a strong foundation in machine learning and artificial intelligence. Born and raised in Brazil, his academic journey reflects a deep passion for exploring innovative AI solutions. His research has focused on neurosymbolic AI and generative text-to-image models, with collaborative projects undertaken with the University of Florida's Florida Institute for National Security and Carnegie Mellon University’s Human-Computer Interaction Institute.

Beyond his research contributions, such as investigations into explainable AI and the development of methods to enhance system transparency and efficiency, Matheus has actively contributed to the University of Florida campus. He served as President of the UF Data Science and Informatics organization, co-founded the UF Signal Processing Society, and held position on the executive boards of both the UF AI and Statistics Clubs, working to expand access to AI education and research. His leadership was recently recognized by his induction into the University of Florida Hall of Fame, the highest recognition given to student leaders at the University.

Following graduation, Matheus will conduct research on AI agents at Microsoft before pursuing a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence Systems at the University of Florida and eventually a PhD in Neurosymbolic AI.
